## Which hypotheses the numbers are consistent with

**H1: F3 feedback loop.** Consistent.
- The candidate F3 of the top-5 vehicles falls from about 0.40 to about 0.01 (S1) and from 0.35 to 0.02–0.03 (S2). The rest stay at 0.79–0.95 (S1) and 0.35–0.76 (S2).
