# TTA estimation on CIFAR-100 (Stage 13 and 13B)

Log-only. Extends `TTA_estimation_report.md` (CIFAR-10) to the 12 CIFAR-100 runs, at the user's request (2026-10-05). Generated by `sumo_open_loop/tta_estimation_c100.py`; per-run values in `tta_estimates_cifar100.csv`.

## What was kept and what was changed

- **Estimators and their parameters are unchanged** (E0 median-11, E1 sustained ×3, E2 MA-5 interpolated, E3 isotonic, E4 saturating fit with RMSE ≤ 1 pt, E5 AUC over 30 min).
- **Targets: 30 / 40 / 50 %**, because 75–80 % is never reached on CIFAR-100 (plateau about 46–51 %). These are the same targets as the Stage 13 reports.
- **The headline estimator stays E2**, fixed on CIFAR-10 before any arm comparison. It is not re-chosen here.
- **Measurement.** One evaluation every 10 rounds on the 10,000-image test set. The binomial SE of one reading is 0.46 / 0.49 / 0.50 points at 30 / 40 / 50 %.
