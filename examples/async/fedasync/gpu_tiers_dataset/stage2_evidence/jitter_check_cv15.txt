Stage 2 - Change A: jitter lowered to a nominal CV of 15%
==============================================================================

sigma = 0.149166  =  sqrt(ln(1 + 0.15^2))
nominal CV = sqrt(exp(sigma^2) - 1) = 15.0000%

Stage 1 used sigma = 0.18 (nominal CV 18.15%). At that value the orin
and thor sleep ranges cleared each other by only 5% in the seed-4126
draw, and orin/thor is exactly the pair the cpu arm already cannot
separate -- an overlap there would shrink the gpu advantage under
measurement rather than reveal it.

SIGMA IS CHOSEN HERE ON THE SEPARATION MARGIN, a property of the fleet,
before any accuracy result exists. It is locked and must not be
revisited once results arrive, whatever they show.

5. N = 30 IS DROPPED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

50/35/15 does not divide into 30 (15 / 10.5 / 4.5). The realised
15/10/5 puts thor -- the FASTEST tier -- at 16.7% instead of 15%, so a
sweep along N would move fleet size and tier mix together at that one
point. The fleet-size sweep is N in {20, 40}, both of which divide
exactly (10/7/3 and 20/14/6).

make_configs.py no longer accepts N = 30: its partition_size entry is
removed and its port slot is left unused rather than reassigned, so an
old N=30 config cannot silently collide with a new run.
