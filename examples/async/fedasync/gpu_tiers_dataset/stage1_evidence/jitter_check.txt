1. sigma, and why this one
------------------------------------------------------------------------
sigma = 0.18, drawn once per client from seed 4126:

    sleep_i = T_tier(tier_i) * exp(eps_i),   eps_i ~ Normal(0, sigma)

The CV of that lognormal is sqrt(exp(sigma^2) - 1) = 18.15%,
which is the middle of the 15-20% the brief asks for. sigma was chosen on that
number and not adjusted afterwards: it is a property of the
distribution, so it does not move with the draw and cannot be tuned
to make a realised statistic land anywhere in particular.

2. realised CV per tier
------------------------------------------------------------------------
tier       n   CV(pop)   CV(smp)   in 15-20%?
xavier    10    16.55%    17.45%   yes
orin       7    14.90%    16.10%   pop below
thor       3    17.25%    21.13%   smp above

Two conventions are given because the tiers are small and the two
differ by sqrt(n/(n-1)) -- 22% at n = 3. Quoting one without saying
which would make the target unfalsifiable.

NOT EVERY NUMBER IS INSIDE THE BAND, and no sigma puts them all there.
In this fleet orin has the lowest realised CV and thor the highest, so
the band is constrained from both sides at once: orin's population CV
reaches 15% only near sigma = 0.17, and thor's sample CV passes 20% at
about the same point, leaving a window roughly 0.001 wide in sigma.
Chasing it would be tuning sigma to make a statistic land somewhere,
which the brief forbids.

These are sampling errors of the estimator at small n, not a statement
about the jitter. The same sigma and the same draw rule give, per tier
and per fleet size (pop / smp):

    N     xavier            orin              thor
    20    16.55 / 17.45     14.90 / 16.10     17.25 / 21.13
    30    15.22 / 15.75     19.26 / 20.30     16.83 / 18.81
    40    17.32 / 17.77     16.47 / 17.09     13.68 / 14.99

Every tier moves several points between fleet sizes, and which tier is
high and which is low changes with them -- thor is the highest at
N = 20 and the lowest at N = 40. That is what n = 3..6 does to a CV.

3. min / mean / max sleep per tier
------------------------------------------------------------------------
tier       n    T_tier       min      mean       max  max/min
xavier    10   15.0000   10.8446   14.2802   17.8947   1.650x
orin       7    1.9231    1.4260    1.7550    2.1976   1.541x
thor       3    1.0000    0.9399    1.0910    1.3564   1.443x

T_tier is the datasheet value and, because exp(eps) has median 1, the
tier's MEDIAN rather than its mean. The mean sits at T_tier *
exp(sigma^2/2) = 1.0163x T_tier in expectation;
what each tier's mean actually did is above, and it is dominated by
the draw, not by that factor, at these fleet sizes.

4. do the tiers still separate?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The requirement: jitter must not blur the tiers. Margin is
slower.min / faster.max, so > 1 means the ranges do not touch.

xavier   [ 10.8446,  17.8947]  vs  orin     [  1.4260,   2.1976]
         -> no overlap, margin 4.935x
orin     [  1.4260,   2.1976]  vs  thor     [  0.9399,   1.3564]
         -> no overlap, margin 1.051x

EXPLICIT STATEMENT: neither xavier/orin nor orin/thor overlaps.

The binding constraint on sigma is orin/thor, not the CV target.
Those two tiers are only 1.92x apart before jitter (1.9231 s against
1.0 s), against 7.8x for xavier/orin. At sigma = 0.20 they overlap in
this draw; at 0.18 they clear by the margin above.
